VIC — Process Anywhere. Capture Anywhere. Record Everything.
**My Dream Is Now Your Dream.**

**Imagine OBS — but instead of building one recording from one computer, you can capture your entire LAN from one central place.**

**VIC — Video Ingest Center** turns every connected PC into a capture worker. From one dashboard, you can discover, preview, record and manage individual applications, windows, monitors, microphones, audio devices, cameras, capture cards, RTSP/IP cameras, network streams, livestreams and media sources across multiple computers.

Every source can be treated independently and recorded to **its own separate file**, rather than everything being permanently mixed into one recording.

Want one game recorded separately from Discord? Done.

Want three applications, two monitors, a microphone and several cameras all recording independently? Done.

Want another PC somewhere else on your LAN doing the actual capture or processing? Done.

You can **process here, capture there, store somewhere else — and control everything from one place.**

Unlike a traditional recorder where you create a single scene or output, VIC is designed around **mass independent capture**. Start one source, twenty sources, or an entire machine's available capture devices at once — limited primarily by the CPU, GPU, storage and network bandwidth available to your cluster.

### **One Dashboard. Every PC. Every Source. Separate Recordings.**
VIC brings together:

- **Application & window capture** — independently record individual programs instead of the whole desktop.
- **Multi-monitor capture** — each screen can become its own recording source.
- **Per-application audio** — keep programs and audio sources separated instead of baking everything together.
- **Microphones & audio devices** — capture individual physical and virtual audio inputs.
- **Cameras & capture cards** — webcams, virtual cameras, USB capture devices and other video hardware.
- **RTSP/IP cameras** — bring network cameras into the same system.
- **Network streams & livestreams** — ingest supported remote video streams alongside local sources.
- **Multiple worker PCs** — distribute capture and processing across computers on your LAN.
- **Independent files** — every source can have its own recording rather than being permanently combined.
- **Live previews** — see what connected sources are producing from the central interface.
- **Central recording control** — start, stop and manage recordings without sitting at the machine doing the capture.
- **Worker/process monitoring** — see which machine is responsible for a source and where its processing is happening.
- **Flexible processing and storage** — capture on one machine, process on another and move recordings to your chosen storage.
- **Mass Capture** — automatically discover a worker's available screens, audio devices and cameras and create multiple independent sources at once.
-**Roll Safe** —  automatically saves and starts recording the moment you click so no loss of fottage every second count if green thne good to go if not then change worker.
